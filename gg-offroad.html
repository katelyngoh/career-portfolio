Growing a Niche Shopify DTC Brand: +62% YoY Net Sales

  • Company: GG Offroad (ggoffroad.com)
  • Role: Co-Founder, Ecommerce & Growth Lead
  • Timeline: Dec 2022 – Present (full-time lead since April 2026)

At a Glance

Challenge
Grow a self-funded DTC brand selling a $200+, install-required product to a niche audience, with minimal paid media.
What I did
Migrated the store to Shopify and built education-led conversion content, community and creator acquisition, post-purchase email, and a B2B distribution channel.
Results
Net sales +62% YoY, AOV +11%, conversion steady at about 3.3%, returning-customer rate nearly doubled.
Skills
Shopify, SEO, paid search, CRO, lifecycle email, creator/affiliate, B2B partnerships, AI automation

Context

  • GG Offroad sells camera relocation kits that restore backup camera and digital mirror views on Toyota trucks after a camper or truck cap is installed.
  • My co-founder engineered the product. I own growth: site, acquisition, conversion, retention, and partnerships.

Acquisition

  • Search is the largest referral source, driving about 36% of sales (paid and organic combined), building on my SEO and paid search leadership at Healthgrades and Red Ventures.
  • Grew community-led demand through value-first Reddit and forum participation and local off-road club events.
  • Launched a creator/affiliate program with off-road YouTubers and a customer referral program.
  • Built a Claude workflow that monitors forums and social media daily and drafts replies for my review.

Conversion

  • Migrated from Squarespace to Shopify in mid-2026 and built the site with Claude.
  • Wrote all product pages, fitment FAQs, and policies.
  • Produced install videos and embedded them on product pages to answer the top objection: "Can I install this myself?"

Retention

  • Built a post-purchase email that guides installation and reduces support load.

B2B and Distribution

  • Built distribution partnerships across the US, Canada, and Australia, plus an installer network that refers customers and buys in bulk.
  • Leading outreach to truck cap manufacturers; negotiating an integration partnership with a major manufacturer.

Results

  • +62%Net sales YoY (Oct 2026 vs Oct 2025), driven by orders up about 50% and AOV up 11% ($207 to $231)
  • ~3.3%Conversion rate, steady at 3.1–3.6% per month on a $200+ product (Shopify, Jun–Oct 2026)
  • 7.6% → 14.3%Returning-customer rate nearly doubled (Jun to Oct)

What's Next

  • Optimize product page to cart, where most drop-off happens
  • Split search into paid vs organic and report CAC by channel

Note: 2025 data is from Squarespace and 2026 from Shopify, so traffic and conversion are compared within Shopify only.
