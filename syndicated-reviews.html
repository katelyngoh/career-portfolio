Protecting Review Stars and Click-Through Across a Partner Network

  • Company: Healthgrades (RVO Health)
  • Role: Associate Director, SEO
  • Timeline: 2024 – 2025

At a Glance

Challenge
Google stopped crediting reviews shared across partner sites, and review stars vanished from most search listings. Removing reviews would have cost traffic and partner revenue.
What I did
Led a cross-functional redesign so each partner site shows unique review content, then grew native reviews on partner sites.
Results
Review submission conversion 5x, review stars roughly doubled, AI Overview citations 7% to 51%.
Skills
Trust signals and reviews, conversion optimization, partner strategy, executive alignment

The Business Problem

  • Healthgrades syndicates provider profiles, including ratings and reviews, to partner sites that rely on them for credibility and revenue.
  • After Google algorithm updates, the identical reviews were treated as duplicate content, and listings showing review stars fell from about 90% to about 10%.
  • Internal data showed review stars lift click-through by about 20%, so the loss hit Healthgrades and partners alike.

What I Did

  • Owned the strategy end to end and secured executive buy-in despite engineering-cost concerns.
  • Designed a model where each partner site displays a unique set of reviews per provider, drawn from the same underlying review data.
  • Worked with partners to distribute office-collected reviews uniquely across the network.
  • Simplified review submission on partner sites, with AI-assisted moderation, so native reviews replace syndicated ones over time.

Why It Matters for Ecommerce

Reviews drive conversion on a product page or a search result alike. This is the same playbook as a DTC review program: protect the stars that drive clicks, make leaving a review easy, and manage channel partners who share the same content.

Results

  • 5xReview submission conversion on partner sites: 0.2% to 1%
  • ~10% → 21%Listings with review stars: roughly doubled since launch and still climbing
  • +13%Provider traffic across the network after launch
  • 7% → 51%Google AI Overview citation rate

Duplicate-content risk resolved without losing partner traffic.
