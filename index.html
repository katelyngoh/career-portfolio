About

I'm Katie Goh, an ecommerce marketer with 12+ years across paid search, paid social, SEO, and email.

At GG Offroad, our bootstrapped Shopify DTC brand, I own marketing end to end: SEO, Google and Meta Ads, email segmentation, a creator/affiliate program, community marketing, and a B2B channel that turns installers and truck cap manufacturers into repeat customers. I cover every other function of the business too, from customer service to fulfillment and accounting.

Before that, I spent 8+ years at Red Ventures and RVO Health, most recently as Associate Director of SEO at Healthgrades, where I led a team and reported weekly to senior leadership.

I build with AI, not just use it. I coded our Shopify site with Claude, run automated Claude tasks that scan forums and social media every morning and draft replies to customer conversations, and built my own to-do list app, Trailhead.
