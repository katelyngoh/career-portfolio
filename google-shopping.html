Launching Google Shopping: 0 to 1,380 Approved Listings in Five Weeks

  • Company: GG Offroad (ggoffroad.com)
  • Role: Co-Founder, Ecommerce & Growth Lead
  • Timeline: Sept – Nov 2026

At a Glance

Challenge
Get a home-based Shopify brand selling niche truck parts into Google's free Shopping listings in 59 countries, with every product blocked on day one.
What I did
Fixed the account-level block, rebuilt the catalog for vehicle-specific search, priced shipping in 50+ local currencies from real checkout quotes, launched a promotion, and recovered from a misrepresentation flag.
Results
1,380 listings approved with 0 limited, 728 Google clicks in the latest 28 days (+174%), and Shopping clicks up from zero, at $0 ad spend.
Skills
Google Merchant Center, Shopify, product feed optimization, international shipping, technical SEO, Shopify GraphQL, AI-assisted operations

Context

  • GG Offroad sells camera relocation kits for Toyota Tacoma and Tundra trucks. Search already drove about a third of sales, but none of the catalog appeared in Google Shopping.
  • We're a two-person, home-based business, so the account had to pass Google's identity and business verification without a storefront.
  • I ran the project with Claude as an operations partner: auditing the feed, editing products through the Shopify API, and working through Merchant Center diagnostics with me.

Unblocking the Account

  • 720 products disapproved, one root cause. Merchant Center had claimed the Shopify subdomain while the feed sent the custom domain. One settings fix cleared every product.
  • Removed a market that required a local business registration we couldn't obtain, which was holding every listing at "Limited."
  • Passed identity verification as a home-based business by keeping the legal name, address, and contact details identical across the site, policies, and Google.

Rebuilding the Catalog for Search

  • One product per truck. Split the two flagship kits into separate Tacoma and Tundra products, so each landing page matches the exact truck a shopper searches for. 301 redirects kept existing links working.
  • Rewrote titles to lead with year and model ("2024+ Toyota Tacoma Digital Rearview Mirror…") and rewrote descriptions to cover every fitment term buyers use.
  • Corrected product categories on nine products, and found a hidden Google category field overriding the fix on five of them.
  • Filled structured attributes (fit type, vehicle type, cable shielding) and added custom labels by truck and product type for future reporting and bidding.
  • Fixed cross-links that sent Tundra shoppers to Tacoma kits, and updated image alt text to match each product.

Shipping in 50+ Currencies

  • Google blocked 233 products in 32 countries because shipping was listed in USD while prices showed in local currency.
  • Priced from real quotes, not guesses. Ran test checkouts to get real FedEx quotes in each currency, then built flat-rate policies padded 20% so a listing never understates the checkout cost.
  • Kept local-currency checkout and prepaid duties through Shopify Managed Markets rather than forcing international buyers into USD.
  • When an app sync silently wiped the hand-built policies, I traced the cause, turned the sync off, and rebuilt them.

Promotion and Trust Signals

  • Rejected a free-shipping promotion after realizing it only changed what customers paid on one low-priced item, and launched a trackable GOOGLE10 offer instead.
  • Synced the return policy, set up Google Customer Reviews on the order confirmation page, and weighed paid review feeds and buy-now-pay-later options on cost versus likely return.

Recovering from a Misrepresentation Flag

  • Google flagged the account for misrepresentation after a burst of changes during its initial review.
  • Audited every place Google cross-checks (business name, address, policies, shipping, promotion), fixed the inconsistencies, then froze changes until the review passed. The account was reinstated.

Results

  • 720 → 0Disapproved products after the account-level fix
  • 1,380Listings approved, 0 limited, across 50+ countries
  • +174%Google clicks in Merchant Center (728 in the latest 28 days)
  • 0 → 28Shopping listing clicks per 28 days, at $0 ad spend

Two of the first three GOOGLE10 orders came through the new Tundra-specific kit page, an early sign that the product split is reaching shoppers the combined page missed.

What I Learned

  • Merchant Center rewards stability. Most of the problems came from making many correct changes at once during review, so I'd now stage changes after approval rather than before.
  • Clicks aren't revenue yet. Merchant Center's click count rose faster than store sessions from Google, which held roughly flat. The listings are live and earning clicks; turning them into measurable revenue lift is the next phase.

What's Next

  • Add installed-on-truck and mirror-view photos, the biggest remaining listing-quality gap
  • Group reviews across the split products so star ratings show on every listing
  • Test a small Performance Max campaign, segmented by the truck and product-type labels already in the feed

Note: Click data is from Google Merchant Center and session data from Shopify Analytics, comparing the 36 days after launch with the 36 days before.
